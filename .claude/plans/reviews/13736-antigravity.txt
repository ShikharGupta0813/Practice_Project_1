[HIGH] application/modules/mobile_app/controllers/apiv6/CrifCreditReport.php:702 - Missing updated_at fallback in canRefresh causes cache bypass
    category=Correctness confidence=0.92
    While `reportInfo()` falls back to `updated_at` when `report_fetched_at` is empty, `canRefresh()` passes an empty string directly to `nextRefreshTime()`. Consequently, `canRefresh()` evaluates to `true` even while `reportInfo()` reports a future `next_refresh_on` timestamp. For inquiries fetched prior to adding `report_fetched_at`, calls to `initiate` will bypass the 30-day cache check and trigger redundant, billable CRIF pulls. Update `canRefresh()` to apply the same `updated_at` fallback to keep caching behavior and response metadata consistent.
