# PART 1 — In simple words

## What the system does today
Our bank customers receive money by UPI (instant payments) through Yes Bank. Every so often a small program (the "worker") asks Yes Bank for its list of payments and compares it with our own records. For each payment it should do one of three things: mark our record as "settled" (money has reached the bank), write the payment down in a "missing payments" list if we never had it, or write it in a "disputed payments" list if it is a dispute. People then work from those lists. When a payment is marked settled, the receiver should also get a confirmation email.

## What is wrong with it
Since August 2025 the worker has been writing in **practice notebooks** instead of the real books. The "missing" and "dispute" lists it writes to are practice copies (`_test` tables) that nothing reads; the practice "missing" copy now holds about 2.2 million rows, while the real list the team uses has only about 23,700. The step that marks a payment as settled is switched off in the code (left with a "revert" note), and the confirmation email is switched off too. Even if switched on as written, they would change a frozen practice copy of the transactions table (7,457 rows, all from one afternoon on 8 Aug 2025), not the real one (about 28.6 million rows). Result: real payments that Yes Bank has settled stay "pending" in our system, missing and disputed payments never reach the people who should chase them, and receivers get no confirmation email.

## What we are going to change
Point both workers back at the real tables, and switch the "mark as settled" step and the confirmation email back on.

## What could go wrong
- The first runs after release will mark many older "pending" payments as settled and send the confirmation emails for them. That is the intended behaviour, but receivers will suddenly get emails, possibly for older payments. The emails cannot repeat for the same payment (there is a "mail already sent" flag), but they will arrive.
- The second worker (`Super`) will start searching the real 28-million-row table. If that search is not indexed it could be slow.
- Missing and disputed payments will start appearing on the real lists staff use, including a few "already settled" ones (see Part 3).
- Rolling back stops further changes but does not un-settle payments or un-send emails.
